# Synthetic University of Technology | Examination Bye-Laws 2024 | BYE
CHAPTER 1: PRELIMINARY
1.1 Applicability. These Bye-Laws govern the conduct of examinations for the B.Tech programme admitted in the 2024 academic year.
1.2 Precedence. In the event of any inconsistency between these Bye-Laws and the Academic Regulations 2024, the matter shall be referred to the Controller of Examinations for a ruling, and neither shall be treated as prevailing until the ruling is issued.
CHAPTER 5: EXAMINATION CONDUCT
5.1 Hall Tickets. A hall ticket shall be issued only to a student who is eligible to appear for the ESE in the course.
5.2 Malpractice. A case of malpractice shall be reported to the Malpractice Committee by the invigilator on the same day.
CHAPTER 8: ATTENDANCE AND ELIGIBILITY FOR EXAMINATIONS
8.1 Attendance Lists. The Examination Section shall receive the final attendance lists of every course from the Head of Department before the issue of hall tickets.
8.2 Medical Certificates. A medical certificate submitted in support of any application relating to attendance shall be submitted within 10 days of the student resuming classes, and shall be verified by the University Health Centre.
8.3 Detention List. The Examination Section shall publish the list of students detained under Clause 3.4 of the Academic Regulations before the commencement of the ESE.
CHAPTER 14: RE-EVALUATION AND REVIEW
14.1 Form of Application. An application for re-evaluation shall be made on the prescribed form to the CoE.
14.2 Scrutiny. A student may inspect the evaluated answer script on application to the CoE.
14.3 Time Limit for Re-evaluation. Applications for re-evaluation shall be received within 7 days of the declaration of results, and applications received later shall be rejected.
CHAPTER 15: GRIEVANCES
15.1 Grade Grievances. A grade grievance shall be addressed to the Grade Grievance Committee within 10 days of the declaration of results.
